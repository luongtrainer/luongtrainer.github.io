Kisato Video Studio

A private desktop production tool for the YouTube channel TỪ ĐƯỜNG KISATO.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  • Helps the channel owner write, edit and render short documentary videos on the owner's own computer.
  • Uploads the finished video to the owner's own YouTube channel, sets the designed thumbnail, and schedules the publish time.
  • Shows the owner how each of their videos performs (views, watch time, retention, shares, comments asking for the free guide) so the next video can be made better.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the channel, on their own computer. The tool is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, no servers and no advertising.

Data and privacy

All data stays on the owner's computer.

Kisato Video Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
